# Email Scripts Library

## 10 Copy-Paste Email Templates for Client Communication

These email templates cover the entire client journey from first contact to final delivery. Customize with your business details and client information.

## Template 1: Initial Outreach Email

**Subject:** Quick question about your [content/brand] 👀

**Body:**

```
Hey [Client Name],

I hope this email finds you well!

I've been following your [content/brand/work] on [platform] and I'm genuinely impressed with [specific thing they do well—be authentic].

I noticed you're [observation: posting regularly but videos are unedited / creating great content but not repurposing / etc.], and I wanted to reach out.

I'm a short-form video editor specializing in [YOUR NICHE—creators/ecom/agencies/coaches], and I help [SEGMENT] scale their content output without spending hours editing.

I'd love to show you some examples of how I've helped [similar clients] turn raw footage into viral-worthy Reels/TikToks/Shorts.

Are you open to a quick 15-minute call this week to explore if we'd be a good fit?

You can book directly here: [Calendly link]
Or just reply with your availability and I'll send a calendar invite.

Either way, keep crushing it with your content!

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Business Name]
[Website]
[Instagram: @yourbusiness]
```

**Customization Notes:**
- Replace [specific thing] with an authentic compliment
- Keep it under 150 words
- Include clear CTA (Calendly link or ask for availability)

## EMAIL BEST PRACTICES

### Subject Lines

**Good:**
- "Your first draft is ready! 🎬"
- "Quick question about your content 👀"
- "Final videos delivered! 🚀"

**Bad:**
- "Re: Re: Re: Re: Project"
- "URGENT: PLEASE READ"
- "Hey" (too vague)

**Tips:**
- Keep under 50 characters
- Use emojis (sparingly—1 per subject line)
- Be specific, not vague

---

### Tone & Voice

**DO:**
- ✅ Be professional but friendly
- ✅ Use emojis to convey warmth (not excessively)
- ✅ Write like you talk (conversational)
- ✅ Use bullet points and formatting (easy to scan)

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Be overly formal ("Dear Sir/Madam")
- ❌ Write long paragraphs (break them up)
- ❌ Use industry jargon (assume they don't know editing terms)
- ❌ Be vague ("Let me know if you have questions"—about what?)

---

### Formatting

**Use:**
- ✅ Headings (---) to break up sections
- ✅ Bullet points for lists
- ✅ Bold for important info (dates, prices, CTAs)
- ✅ Emojis for visual breaks (✅ ❌ 🎬 📁 🚀)

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Walls of text
- ❌ Tiny fonts (use default size)
- ❌ Excessive colors/fonts (looks spammy)

---

### Timing

**When to Send:**

| Email Type | Best Time |
|------------|-----------|
| Outreach | Tuesday-Thursday, 10 AM - 2 PM |
| Follow-up | 3 days after no reply, 9-11 AM |
| Draft delivery | Morning (gives them all day to review) |
| Payment reminder | Monday morning (start of week) |
| Testimonial request | Weekend (when they have time) |

**Avoid:**
- ❌ Monday 8 AM (inbox overload)
- ❌ Friday 5 PM (weekend mode)
- ❌ Late night (looks desperate)

---

### Response Time Expectations

**Set clear expectations:**
- "I respond within 24 hours (usually much faster)"
- "If urgent, text me at [number]"

**Your standard:**
- Respond to inquiries within 1-4 hours (business hours)
- Respond to client questions within 24 hours
- Acknowledge receipt even if you don't have an answer yet
