# Asset Collection Checklist

## Complete Guide to Requesting & Organizing Client Assets

This checklist ensures you collect everything you need from clients upfront, preventing delays and back-and-forth during editing.

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS

**Poor asset collection = project delays, frustration, and scope creep.**

**Good asset collection = smooth workflow, happy clients, on-time delivery.**

---

# ASSET REQUEST WORKFLOW

## Phase 1: Initial Request (After Contract Signed)

Send the Asset Request Email (see 08-email-scripts.md, Template 4) within 1 hour of receiving deposit.

**Include:**
- [ ] Google Drive folder link (pre-organized with subfolders)
- [ ] Clear list of what to upload
- [ ] Deadline (24-48 hours)
- [ ] Quality guidelines (resolution, format, etc.)

---

## Phase 2: Folder Setup (Before Client Upload)

### Create Google Drive Folder Structure

**Folder Name:** `[Client Name] - [Project Name] - [Date]`

**Subfolders:**

```
[Client Name] Project/
├── 01 - Raw Footage/
├── 02 - Brand Assets/
│   ├── Logos/
│   ├── Fonts/
│   └── Brand Guidelines/
├── 03 - Scripts & Notes/
├── 04 - Music & Audio/
├── 05 - Reference Videos/
├── 06 - Work In Progress/
└── 07 - Finals/
```

**Folder Permissions:**
- Set to: "Anyone with the link can upload"
- Or: Share directly with client's email (Editor access)

---

## Phase 3: Asset Review (After Client Upload)

Within 24 hours of client upload, review all assets:

- [ ] All files uploaded?
- [ ] File quality acceptable (resolution, audio, lighting)?
- [ ] Any missing assets?
- [ ] Any questions or clarifications needed?

**If issues found:**
- Send polite email requesting missing/corrected assets
- Adjust timeline if delay is significant

**If all good:**
- Reply: "Got everything! Starting work today. First draft by [Date]."

# ASSET CHECKLIST BY CATEGORY

## 1. RAW FOOTAGE

### What to Request:

- [ ] **All raw video clips**
  - Format: MOV or MP4 (preferred)
  - Resolution: Minimum 1080p (1920x1080)
  - Orientation: Vertical (1080x1920) for TikTok/Reels
  - Frame rate: 24fps, 30fps, or 60fps

- [ ] **Multiple takes** (if available)
  - Gives you options to choose best performance
  - Allows for A/B testing different hooks

- [ ] **B-roll footage**
  - Hands, products, environment shots
  - Cutaways to cover edits
  - Visual variety

### Quality Standards:

**✅ GOOD:**
- Well-lit (natural light or ring light)
- Clear audio (built-in mic okay, external mic better)
- Steady camera (tripod or handheld with stabilization)
- Subject in focus

**❌ UNUSABLE:**
- Blurry or out of focus
- Audio too quiet or distorted
- Extremely shaky (can't fix in post)
- Wrong orientation (horizontal when you need vertical)

**If footage is unusable:**
- Notify client immediately
- Explain what's wrong and how to re-film
- Offer guidance: "Use a ring light, film in portrait mode, speak louder"
- Adjust timeline if re-filming is needed

## 2. BRAND ASSETS

### Logos

- [ ] **Logo files (PNG with transparent background)**
  - Full logo (with text)
  - Icon/symbol only (if applicable)
  - White version (for dark backgrounds)
  - Black version (for light backgrounds)
  - Resolution: At least 1000x1000px

**If they don't have PNG:**
- Request JPG (you can remove background using remove.bg)
- Or create simple text logo using their brand name

### Brand Colors

- [ ] **Color palette (hex codes)**
  - Primary color: #XXXXXX
  - Secondary color: #XXXXXX
  - Accent color: #XXXXXX
  - Background: #XXXXXX
  - Text: #XXXXXX

**If they don't know hex codes:**
- Ask for screenshot of their website/Instagram
- Use color picker tool (imagecolorpicker.com)
- Or: "What colors represent your brand?" (e.g., "Blue and gold")

### Fonts

- [ ] **Brand fonts (if specific)**
  - Font name (e.g., "Montserrat Bold")
  - Where to download (Google Fonts, Adobe Fonts)
  - License (are they allowed to use it commercially?)

**If they don't have specific fonts:**
- Use safe defaults: Montserrat, Poppins, Helvetica, Arial
- Choose based on their brand vibe (bold, playful, professional)

### Brand Guidelines (Optional)

- [ ] **Brand style guide PDF** (if they have one)
  - Tone of voice
  - Do's and don'ts
  - Visual style examples

**Most small clients won't have this—that's okay.**

---

## 3. SCRIPTS & TALKING POINTS

### What to Request:

- [ ] **Script (if they're reading from one)**
  - Word-for-word what they say in the video
  - Helps with captioning and editing pacing

- [ ] **Talking points / outline**
  - Key messages to emphasize
  - CTA (what action do they want viewers to take?)

- [ ] **Hooks & CTAs**
  - Opening hook (first 3 seconds)
  - Closing CTA ("Follow for more," "Link in bio," etc.)

**If they didn't script it:**
- No problem—you'll transcribe during editing
- Use auto-captions (Premiere, CapCut, Descript)

---

## 4. MUSIC & AUDIO

### What to Request:

- [ ] **Preferred music/audio**
  - Specific tracks they want (Spotify links, YouTube links)
  - Genre/vibe (upbeat, chill, dramatic)
  - Any tracks to AVOID

- [ ] **Audio files** (if they have custom music)
  - Format: MP3 or WAV
  - Licensed (they have rights to use it)

**Music Sources:**

If they don't provide music, use:
- ✅ **Epidemic Sound** (license: $15/month)
- ✅ **Artlist** (license: $9.99/month)
- ✅ **TikTok/IG audio library** (free, trending sounds)
- ❌ **Copyrighted music** (YouTube, Spotify) = copyright strikes

**Pro Tip:**
- For TikTok/IG: Use trending audio (boosts algorithm)
- For YouTube: Use licensed music (avoids copyright claims)
- For ads: Use royalty-free only (Epidemic, Artlist)

---

## 5. REFERENCE VIDEOS

### What to Request:

- [ ] **3-5 example videos they LOVE**
  - Links to TikToks, Reels, YouTube videos
  - What they like about each one (pacing, effects, style)

- [ ] **1-2 videos they DON'T like**
  - What to avoid (over-editing, slow pacing, etc.)

**Why this matters:**
- Prevents "I don't like it" without specifics
- Aligns expectations on style
- Saves revision rounds

**If they can't find examples:**
- Show them your portfolio and ask: "Which style resonates?"
- Or: "Do you want fast-paced (Mr. Beast) or slower storytelling (Casey Neistat)?"

# ASSET ORGANIZATION BEST PRACTICES

## File Naming Convention

**Use this format:**
`[ClientName]_[AssetType]_[Description]_[Date]`

**Examples:**
- `JohnDoe_RawFootage_Intro_2025-01-15.mov`
- `AcmeBrand_Logo_Transparent_2025-01-15.png`
- `SarahCoach_BRoll_Workspace_2025-01-15.mp4`

**Why this matters:**
- Easy to find files later
- No confusion if you have multiple clients
- Professional organization

---

## Version Control

When delivering drafts:

**Version naming:**
- `ClientName_VideoName_V1.mp4` (first draft)
- `ClientName_VideoName_V2.mp4` (after revisions)
- `ClientName_VideoName_FINAL.mp4` (approved)

**Never overwrite previous versions:**
- Keeps history in case client changes mind
- Easy to compare versions

---

## Backup Strategy

**CRITICAL: Always backup client assets and your work.**

**Backup locations:**
1. **Google Drive** (primary, shared with client)
2. **External hard drive** (secondary, offline backup)
3. **Cloud storage** (Dropbox, OneDrive, or Backblaze)

**Backup schedule:**
- After each draft delivery
- Before final delivery
- Monthly archive of completed projects

**Retention:**
- Keep for at least 6 months after project completion
- Longer if client might return for more work

# TROUBLESHOOTING COMMON ISSUES

## Issue: Client uploads wrong file format

**Solution:**
- Convert using free tools (HandBrake, CloudConvert)
- Or ask client to re-export from their phone/camera

## Issue: File size too large (can't upload to Drive)

**Solution:**
- Ask client to use WeTransfer (free up to 2GB)
- Or compress video (reduce resolution to 1080p)
- Or split into smaller files

## Issue: Client doesn't have brand assets

**Solution:**
- Extract logo from their social media profile pictures
- Use color picker tool on their website/IG feed
- Create simple text logo if they have no logo

## Issue: Footage quality is terrible

**Solution:**
- Be honest: "This footage is unusable due to [issue]. Can you re-film?"
- Offer guidance: Send them a "How to Film Good Footage" guide
- If they can't re-film: Do your best, set expectations low

## Issue: Client is slow to upload assets

**Solution:**
- Send reminder email (Day 3)
- Send second reminder (Day 7): "Timeline is paused until I receive assets"
- Adjust delivery timeline accordingly
- If it drags on: Consider canceling project (per your contract)

# FINAL CHECKLIST (Before Starting Edit)

Before you open Premiere/Final Cut/CapCut:

- [ ] All raw footage uploaded and reviewed
- [ ] Brand assets (logos, colors, fonts) confirmed
- [ ] Music preferences noted (or sourced)
- [ ] Reference videos reviewed (understand style)
- [ ] Client's goals and CTA clarified
- [ ] Platform specs confirmed (1080x1920 for TikTok/Reels)
- [ ] Deadline confirmed (first draft due [Date])
- [ ] Revision limits reviewed (you know how many rounds they get)

**If ANY of these are missing or unclear:**
- Don't start editing yet
- Reach out to client for clarification
- Better to ask now than deliver the wrong thing

---

# DELIVERABLE CHECKLIST (After Editing)

When uploading finals to Google Drive:

- [ ] All videos exported in correct resolution
- [ ] Platform-specific versions (if applicable)
- [ ] Subtitle files (.SRT) included (if in package)
- [ ] Thumbnail images (if in package)
- [ ] Raw project files (if Silver/Gold package)
- [ ] Files organized in "07 - Finals" folder
- [ ] Files named clearly (VideoName_Platform_FINAL.mp4)

**Then:**
- [ ] Send final delivery email (see 08-email-scripts.md, Template 7)
- [ ] Request final payment
- [ ] Request testimonial (after payment received)
- [ ] Archive project folder (backup, then remove from active Drive)
