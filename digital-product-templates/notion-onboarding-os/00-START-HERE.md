# START HERE - Setup Guide

## Welcome to the Short-Form Editor Client Onboarding OS

This comprehensive system will help you streamline client onboarding, project management, and delivery for your short-form video editing business.

## Setup Steps (Follow in Order)

### Step 1: Duplicate the Notion Template (15 min)

1. **Open the Notion template link** (provided in your purchase)
2. **Click "Duplicate"** in the top right corner
3. **Select your workspace** where you want to install it
4. **Rename** the duplicated template to match your business name

### Step 2: Customize Your Notion Workspace (30 min)

#### A. Update Branding
- Replace "[Your Business Name]" with your actual business name
- Update the logo/icon at the top of the main dashboard
- Customize colors to match your brand (optional)

#### B. Configure Databases
The template includes these pre-built databases:
- **Clients** - All client information and contact details
- **Projects** - Active and completed video projects
- **Assets** - Client-provided footage, logos, brand guidelines
- **Deliverables** - Final videos and revisions
- **Templates** - Reusable email and proposal templates

#### C. Set Up Automation (if using Notion AI)
- Enable notifications for new projects
- Set up reminders for delivery deadlines
- Configure status update alerts

### Step 3: Import Email Templates (10 min)

1. Open the **Email Scripts** folder from your download
2. Copy each template into Notion's Templates database
3. Customize with your:
   - Business name
   - Contact information
   - Pricing details
   - Delivery timelines

### Step 4: Set Up Pricing Calculator (15 min)

1. Open Google Sheets
2. Create a new spreadsheet named "Client Pricing Calculator"
3. Follow the **Pricing Calculator Spec** to build:
   - Base package pricing
   - Add-on services
   - Rush fee calculations
   - Discount logic

### Step 5: Prepare Client-Facing Documents (20 min)

From the **Proposal + Policies** folder:
1. Copy the proposal template into a Google Doc
2. Customize for your business
3. Save 3 versions for Bronze/Silver/Gold packages
4. Add your policies document to your "Legal" folder

### Step 6: Create Your Kickoff Process (15 min)

1. Copy the **Kickoff Questionnaire** into a Google Form OR keep as Notion form
2. Customize questions for your target segment
3. Set up automatic delivery after form submission
4. Test the entire flow with a dummy client

## Recommended Workflow

### When You Get a New Lead:

1. **Send Initial DM/Email** (use script from Email Templates)
2. **Schedule Discovery Call** (15-20 min)
3. **Send Proposal** (Bronze/Silver/Gold options)
4. **Collect 50% Deposit** (via Stripe/PayPal)
5. **Send Kickoff Questionnaire**
6. **Create Project in Notion** (auto-populated from form)
7. **Request Assets** (use Assets Checklist)
8. **Begin Editing**
9. **Deliver Preview** (collect feedback)
10. **Revisions** (per package limits)
11. **Final Delivery** (collect remaining 50%)
12. **Request Testimonial**

## Common Questions

**Q: Do I need Notion premium?**
A: No, the free plan works great for solo editors. Consider upgrading when you have 5+ active clients.

**Q: Can I customize the template?**
A: Absolutely! The template is yours to modify. We encourage you to make it fit your exact workflow.

**Q: What if I use Final Cut Pro instead of Premiere?**
A: The system is editing-software agnostic. Just update references to match your tools.

**Q: How do I handle rush orders?**
A: Use the Pricing Calculator to add 50-100% rush fee depending on timeline.

**Q: What about refunds?**
A: The Scope & Revisions Policy includes refund terms. Typically, deposits are non-refundable once work begins.
