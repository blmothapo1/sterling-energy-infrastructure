# REVISION POLICY

## What Counts as a "Revision"?

We want you to LOVE the final product. Here's how revisions work:

### Minor Revisions (Included per package limits)

**Minor revisions** are small tweaks that don't require re-editing from scratch:

✅ **Examples of Minor Revisions:**
- Change text/caption wording
- Swap background music
- Adjust timing of a cut (move a clip 1-2 seconds)
- Change color of text overlays
- Replace one clip with another (same length)
- Adjust audio levels
- Export in different resolution/format

**Turnaround for Minor Revisions:** 12-24 hours

### Major Revisions (Limited or chargeable)

**Major revisions** are substantial changes that require significant re-work:

⚠️ **Examples of Major Revisions:**
- Completely new edit/concept (different hook, structure, narrative)
- Adding new footage that changes the story
- Changing video length by 30+ seconds
- Switching platforms (e.g., horizontal to vertical)
- Re-doing motion graphics or animations
- Adding new sections or scenes
- Changing the entire music track after multiple minor music swaps

**Major Revision Policy:**
- **Bronze:** Not included (requires upgrade or new project quote)
- **Silver:** 1 major revision included; additional major revisions = $97 each
- **Gold:** 2 major revisions included; additional major revisions = $67 each

**Turnaround for Major Revisions:** 48-72 hours

## Revision Request Process

To ensure fast turnaround and clarity:

### How to Request Revisions:

1. **Review the draft** within 48 hours of delivery
2. **Provide consolidated feedback** (don't send multiple separate emails)
3. **Use timestamps** (e.g., "At 0:15, change text to...")
4. **Be specific** (not "make it pop"—instead, "add a zoom effect at 0:20")
5. **Submit via:**
   - Notion comments (preferred)
   - Loom video with timestamps
   - Email with detailed notes

### We Will NOT Accept:
- ❌ Vague feedback ("I don't like it" without specifics)
- ❌ Contradictory feedback from multiple stakeholders (get aligned internally first)
- ❌ Requests after you've already approved the video (see "Final Approval" below)
