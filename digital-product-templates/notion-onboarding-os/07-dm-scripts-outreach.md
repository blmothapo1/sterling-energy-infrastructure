# DM Scripts & Outreach Schedule

## 7-Day Outreach Plan to Get Your First 3 Clients

**Goal:** 30 DMs per day = 210 total outreach contacts/week  
**Target Close Rate:** 5-10% (10-21 discovery calls → 3-6 clients)

---

## TARGET AUDIENCE IDENTIFICATION

### Where to Find Your Ideal Clients

#### For CREATORS Segment:
- [ ] Instagram: Search #contentcreator #microinfluencer (10K-100K followers)
- [ ] TikTok: Filter by 50K-500K followers, posting 3-5x/week
- [ ] YouTube: Channels with 10K-100K subs, regular shorts/reels
- [ ] Twitter: Creators tweeting about "growing my brand"

**Red Flags to Avoid:**
- ❌ Already has a video editor in bio
- ❌ Posting professional, highly-edited content (they have a team)
- ❌ Less than 5K followers (can't afford you yet)

#### For ECOM BRANDS Segment:
- [ ] Instagram: Search #shopsmallbusiness #dtcbrand
- [ ] Shopify store directory (look for 100-500 products)
- [ ] Facebook Business Pages (local boutiques, online stores)
- [ ] LinkedIn: DTC founders, ecom entrepreneurs

**Good Indicators:**
- ✅ Posting product photos but NOT video content
- ✅ Running ads (check Facebook Ad Library)
- ✅ Active Instagram but low engagement (need better content)
- ✅ Mention "struggling with marketing" in bio/posts

#### For AGENCIES Segment:
- [ ] LinkedIn: Search "marketing agency" "creative agency"
- [ ] Agency directories (Clutch, UpWork top agencies)
- [ ] Instagram: Agencies with 1K-10K followers
- [ ] Twitter: Marketing agency founders

**Good Indicators:**
- ✅ Offering "social media marketing" but no video samples
- ✅ Team size: 3-20 people (need white-label help)
- ✅ Client roster on website (they have budget)

#### For COACHES Segment:
- [ ] Instagram: #businesscoach #lifecoach #fitnesscoach
- [ ] LinkedIn: Career coaches, executive coaches
- [ ] TikTok: Coaches posting tips but rough editing
- [ ] YouTube: Coaches with webinars but no short-form content

**Good Indicators:**
- ✅ Selling a course, program, or coaching package
- ✅ Posting talking-head content (raw, unedited)
- ✅ Mention "scaling my business" "growing my reach"

## DM SCRIPT LIBRARY

### Script #1: The Value-First Opener (Best for Cold Outreach)

**Platform:** Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn

```
Hey [Name]! 👋

I've been following your content on [topic] and noticed you're crushing it with [specific thing they do well].

Quick thought: Have you considered repurposing your content into short-form videos for TikTok/Reels? I help [SEGMENT] turn long-form content into viral-worthy clips.

Not a sales pitch—just genuinely think your [content type] would perform insanely well in short-form.

Would you be open to a quick 15-min call where I walk you through what that could look like?

[Your Name]
[Your Business Name]
```

**Customization Examples:**

*For Creator:*
> "I've been following your fitness tips and your storytelling is 🔥. Have you considered cutting your YouTube videos into Reels? I help fitness creators turn 1 long video into 10+ viral clips."

*For Ecom Brand:*
> "I noticed you're running a DTC skincare brand—love the aesthetic! Are you using short-form video ads? I help ecom brands create UGC-style Reels that convert like crazy."

*For Agency:*
> "Saw you're a marketing agency helping B2B SaaS companies. Do you offer short-form video editing for your clients? I white-label for agencies—happy to be your behind-the-scenes editor."

*For Coach:*
> "Your LinkedIn tips are super valuable! Have you thought about cutting these into 30-second Reels? I help coaches turn their long-form wisdom into bite-sized viral content."

---

### Script #2: The Compliment + Question (Lower Pressure)

**Platform:** Instagram, Twitter

```
[Name]! Just binged your last 5 posts.

Question: Are you editing your videos yourself, or do you have an editor?

Either way, I might have some ideas to help you scale your content output without burning out.

Mind if I share? 👀
```

**Why This Works:**
- Low commitment (just a question)
- Doesn't assume they need help
- Piques curiosity ("ideas to help")

**Follow-Up After They Respond:**

*If they say "I edit myself":*
> "Respect! That takes a lot of time though. What if you could batch-record 10 videos in one day and I turn them into 30 polished Reels? You'd free up 10+ hours/week. Want to see examples of how I've done this for [similar person]?"

*If they say "I have an editor":*
> "Nice! Are you happy with turnaround time and cost? I work with [SEGMENT] on a retainer basis—fast turnaround, unlimited revisions. If you're ever looking for backup or want to scale, hit me up."

---

### Script #3: The Case Study (Social Proof Heavy)

**Platform:** LinkedIn, Email, Instagram

```
Hey [Name],

I just helped [Similar Creator/Brand] grow from 10K to 50K followers in 90 days by editing their raw footage into scroll-stopping short-form content.

They went from posting 2x/week to 5x/week (without filming more content—just repurposed what they already had).

I'm taking on 2 more clients this month. Interested in learning how this could work for you?

Here's a sample: [Link to portfolio/sample]

[Your Name]
```

**Customization:**
- Replace [Similar Creator/Brand] with someone in their niche
- Include specific metrics (growth %, engagement rate, sales)
- Link to your portfolio or a sample edit

### Script #4: The Paid Sample Offer (Higher Conversion)

**Platform:** Instagram, Email

```
Hey [Name]!

I'm a short-form video editor building my portfolio in the [NICHE] space.

Here's the deal: Send me 1 piece of raw footage (unedited) and I'll turn it into a Reels/TikTok/Shorts for FREE.

No strings attached. If you love it, we can talk about working together. If not, you got a free video. Win-win.

Down?
```

**Why This Works:**
- Zero risk for them
- Shows your skills immediately
- High conversion (if your work is good)

**Important:**
- Only do this for first 3-5 clients (portfolio building)
- Set a deadline (deliver in 24 hours to impress)
- Use it to collect testimonials

## 7-DAY OUTREACH SCHEDULE

**Goal:** Identify 150 prospects

**Tasks:**
- [ ] Spend 2 hours researching prospects (use criteria above)
- [ ] Create a spreadsheet:
  - Column A: Name
  - Column B: Platform (IG, TikTok, LinkedIn)
  - Column C: Handle (@username)
  - Column D: Notes (why they're a good fit)
  - Column E: Date contacted
  - Column F: Status (No reply / Replied / Discovery call / Closed)

**Tools:**
- Instagram search (hashtags, location tags)
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator (free trial)
- TikTok search (filter by follower count)
- Google: "[your city] small businesses" + Instagram

---

### Tuesday-Saturday (Days 2-6): Daily Outreach

**Goal:** 30 DMs per day

**Morning Routine (30 min):**
- [ ] Send 15 DMs (use Script #1 or #2)
- [ ] Personalize each message (mention specific post, product, content)
- [ ] Track in spreadsheet

**Evening Routine (30 min):**
- [ ] Send 15 more DMs
- [ ] Respond to any replies from morning batch
- [ ] Update spreadsheet

**DM Distribution:**
- 50% Script #1 (Value-First Opener)
- 30% Script #2 (Compliment + Question)
- 20% Script #3 or #4 (Case Study / Free Sample)

**Pro Tips:**
- Don't copy/paste identically (Instagram flags as spam)
- Wait 30-60 seconds between DMs
- Engage with their content first (like, comment) then DM

## DISCOVERY CALL SCRIPT

**Duration:** 15-20 minutes

### 1. Intro (2 min)

"Hey [Name]! Thanks for hopping on. I know you're busy, so I'll keep this quick.

I help [SEGMENT] create scroll-stopping short-form content without spending hours editing.

Before I share how, tell me—what's your current content workflow? Are you editing yourself or working with someone?"

### 2. Pain Point Identification (5 min)

Ask:
- "How much time are you spending on editing each week?"
- "What's your biggest frustration with your current process?"
- "If you could wave a magic wand, what would your ideal content workflow look like?"

**Listen for:**
- Time constraints ("I spend 10 hours/week editing")
- Quality concerns ("My edits look amateur")
- Scalability issues ("I want to post more but can't keep up")
- Cost concerns ("I can't afford a full-time editor")

### 3. Present Solution (5 min)

"Got it. So here's how I can help:

I offer 3 packages—Bronze, Silver, Gold—designed for exactly this.

Most of my clients start with [Silver], which includes [details].

The way it works: You send me raw footage, I turn it into [X] polished videos per week, you review and request changes, then I deliver finals. Super simple.

Does that sound like something that could work for you?"

### 4. Handle Objections (3 min)

**Objection: "I can't afford it right now."**
Response: "Totally get it. What's your budget? I might be able to create a custom package, or we could start small with Bronze and scale up later."

**Objection: "I need to think about it."**
Response: "For sure! What specific concerns do you have? Happy to address those now so you can make an informed decision."

**Objection: "I already have an editor."**
Response: "Nice! Are you happy with the turnaround and quality? I'm always here as a backup if you need to scale or if they get busy."

### 5. Close (2 min)

**If they're ready:**
"Awesome! I'll send over the proposal and contract today. Once you sign and send the deposit, we can kick off this week. Sound good?"

**If they need time:**
"No problem! I'll send the proposal over today—review it and let me know if you have questions. I'm taking on just 2 more clients this month, so if you're interested, let me know by [Friday]."

**Always:**
- [ ] Send proposal within 24 hours
- [ ] Follow up in 3 days if no response
- [ ] Mark in spreadsheet: Discovery call → Proposal sent → Closed (or Lost)

## RESPONSE HANDLING

### If They Say "Yes, Let's Talk"

1. **Schedule call ASAP** (use Calendly or propose 2-3 time slots)
2. **Send calendar invite** with Zoom/Google Meet link
3. **Prepare:** Review their content, prep talking points
4. **Follow up** day before: "Looking forward to our call tomorrow at [time]!"

### If They Say "Maybe / Not Sure"

```
No worries! Here's a quick example of my work: [portfolio link]

And here's what a typical client process looks like: [1-page PDF or Notion page]

If it resonates, let me know. If not, totally cool—best of luck with your content! 🙂
```

### If They Say "Not Interested"

```
All good! Thanks for the reply. 

If anything changes or you know someone who might benefit, feel free to send them my way.

Good luck with [their business/content]! 🚀
```

**Then:**
- Mark as "Not Interested" in spreadsheet
- Move on (don't chase)
- Follow up in 3-6 months (circumstances change)

### If They Ghost (No Reply)

**Follow-Up Timeline:**
- Day 3: Send follow-up DM (Script #2 or casual check-in)
- Day 7: Final follow-up ("Just circling back...")
- Day 14+: Mark as "No reply" and move on

**Don't:**
- ❌ Send more than 2 follow-ups (comes off desperate)
- ❌ Get defensive or passive-aggressive
- ❌ Spam their posts with comments

## OUTREACH DO'S & DON'TS

### DO:
- ✅ Personalize every message (mention their content)
- ✅ Lead with value (not "I need clients")
- ✅ Keep it short (3-5 sentences max)
- ✅ Include a clear CTA ("Want to see examples?")
- ✅ Follow up (once or twice)
- ✅ Be friendly, not salesy

### DON'T:
- ❌ Copy/paste the same message 100 times
- ❌ Lead with pricing ("I charge $X")
- ❌ Be pushy or aggressive
- ❌ Send essays (keep it concise)
- ❌ Ignore their content (shows you don't care)
- ❌ Get discouraged by rejections (part of the game)

## MINDSET FOR OUTREACH

**This is a numbers game:**
- 210 DMs → 42 replies → 8 calls → 2-3 clients

**That's normal.** Don't take it personally.

**Reframe rejection:**
- "Not interested" = They're not your ideal client anyway
- "Can't afford it" = They'll refer someone who can
- No reply = They're busy, not a reflection of your value

**Stay consistent:**
- 30 DMs/day for 7 days = results
- 5 DMs/day sporadically = no results

**Celebrate small wins:**
- First reply? 🎉
- First call booked? 🎉
- First client? 🎉🎉🎉

## WEEK 2+ STRATEGY

Once you have 1-3 clients, shift strategy:

**Referrals > Cold Outreach**
- Ask every client for 2-3 referrals
- Offer referral incentive ($50 credit)

**Content Marketing > DMs**
- Post case studies on Instagram
- Share before/after edits
- Tag clients (with permission)

**Inbound > Outbound**
- People will start DM'ing YOU
- Increase prices as demand grows
